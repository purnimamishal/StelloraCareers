# StelloraCareers
Built for the student who stays up wondering 'what's next?' a purpose - driven platform to democratize

#### The Real Problem
Many students choose a career not because they want to, not because they are interested in it, and not because they have the skills for it — but because that career is hyped, because they don't know which field to choose, and because of pressure from family and society. This leads to confusion, burnout, and regret after 12th grade.

#### The Solution — StelloraCareers
A platform that helps students cut through the hype and pressure to find clarity based on who they truly are — their interests, skills, and strengths.

#### Vision
To build India's most honest career guidance platform — one that listens to students, not trends.
